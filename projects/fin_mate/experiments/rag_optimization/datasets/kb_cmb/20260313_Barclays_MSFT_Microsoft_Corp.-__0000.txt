Microsoft Corp.
Evolving AI Offering Brings E7 to
Office Range
As part of several product announcements, MSFT today
introduced an E7 SKU to its Office line-up. E7 will combine the
E5 capabilities with Copilot and Agent 365, and represents a
clear up-sell opportunity. We like this move as it harmonizes
the fragmenting Office plus Add-On landscape. 
The Office Suite is one of MSFT's largest franchises, with over 400mn users. Seat growth has
been one growth factor but upside here is limited (mostly entry level expansions), and hence
capturing greater value has been the more important driver of growth.
