Valuation
We value Microsoft using a P/E-based methodology as we believe earnings and earnings growth 
are the most important financial factors for the company, and well reflect Microsoft’s strategy and 
execution.
We roll over our target P/E valuation to FY27. We currently value MSFT at 34x FY27E P/E, vs 40x 
FY26E P/E previously. We remain constructive about Microsoft’s earnings growth sustainability and 
AI leadership among global tech companies.
