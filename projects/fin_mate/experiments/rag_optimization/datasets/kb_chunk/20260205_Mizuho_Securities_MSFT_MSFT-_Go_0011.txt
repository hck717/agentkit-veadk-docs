The strength this quarter was
predominantly driven by Azure commitments from OpenAI, as well as Anthropic, and with healthy
growth across its core annuity sales motions. More specifically, this includes the incremental
$250B in Azure commitments from the recent OpenAI agreement.
Despite continued high capex, reported gross margins were very good at 68.0%, easily above our
67.0% target and the Street's ~67.1% forecast. Further, OMs of 47.1% were much better than
expected and easily surpassed our and the Street's 45.3% target. In addition, under the equity
method of accounting, OpenAI contributed a $9.978B gain (vs. a $4.061B loss previously) to Other
Income & Expense (OI&E), and a $7.583B net gain (vs. $3.086B net loss previously). Non-GAAP
EPS - excluding this component - was $4.14 vs. our $3.96 estimate and the Street's $3.91 forecast.
Finally, CFFO of $35.8B came in well above the Street's $30.4B estimate.
