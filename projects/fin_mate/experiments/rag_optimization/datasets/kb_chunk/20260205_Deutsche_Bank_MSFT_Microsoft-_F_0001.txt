From a margin and LTV perspective we believe the application layer has and is still 
generally perceived as highly strategic ground on which Microsoft has incumbent 
advantages. But we'd argue it is also the last area to see tangible benefits as AI 
monetization works its way up the stack, and is more than ever viewed as at risk of 
disruption and/or devaluation, which is weighing on sentiment. That story has yet 
to play out in our view, so while foregoing Azure revenue now for high-margin 
application and consumer SW opportunity ahead seems a sensible long-term 
strategy, it puts the onus on Microsoft to deliver before it will get credit.
