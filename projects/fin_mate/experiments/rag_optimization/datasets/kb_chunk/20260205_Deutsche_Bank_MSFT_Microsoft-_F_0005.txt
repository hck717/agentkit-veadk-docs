What We Liked / What We're watching on the Quarter
Revenue/Bookings
(+) F2Q revenue of $81.3bn was 1.5% above the midpoint of guidance, with upside 
concentrated in PBP & IC segments. On a constant currency basis, growth ticked 
down ~1.5pts sequentially to +15% y/y.
(+) Commercial Bookings rose +228% y/y @cc. This was primarily driven by the 
previously disclosed larger Azure commit from OpenAI, alongside benefit from the 
recent Anthropic commitment and "healthy growth" across core annuity sales 
motions on a lower expiry base when adjusted for OpenAI contracts in the prior 
year. Commercial RPO ended the quarter +110% y/y to $625bn; ~45% of the total 
balance is now attributed to commits from OpenAI, with the remaining core 55% 
growing +28% y/y. Total Commercial cRPO now stands at +39% y/y, up from +21% 
a year prior.
