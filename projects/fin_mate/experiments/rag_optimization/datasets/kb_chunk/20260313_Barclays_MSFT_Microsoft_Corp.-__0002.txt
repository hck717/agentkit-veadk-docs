At the time, it seemed MSFT wanted to make Copilot available to a
broader audience (not just the high-end SKU customers) and hence the separate pricing.
However, it does create friction in the sales process, especially for the enterprise level, if two
different pricing agreements (an Office SKU and the Copilot commitment) and discount levels
need to be discussed. In this respect, the new E7 offerings is just a natural extension. E7 includes
E5 plus Copilot and Agent 365. At $99 per user, the offering is above the $60 E5 price (price
update on July 2026) but is cheaper than the individual components (E5 $60 plus Copilot $30
plus Agent 365 $15). We note that in recent quarters, E5 was mentioned as the main growth
driver for Office growth, followed by Copilot.
With its product announcements today, Microsoft also provides an answer for another recent
investor discussion point around the evolution of work.
