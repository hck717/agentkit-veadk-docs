Risks to Our Price Target and Rating for MSFT
Downside risks to our price target include: (1) Competition—Increasing competition, particularly from large tech vendors such as Google, 
Facebook, Amazon, among others; (2) Increasing regulatory scrutiny—Technology companies have increasingly seen scrutiny from 
regulatory agencies and governments globally over the past several years, specifically, antitrust concerns; (3) Cybersecurity Risks; and (4) 
Margin compression—from Azure/OpenAI investments.
