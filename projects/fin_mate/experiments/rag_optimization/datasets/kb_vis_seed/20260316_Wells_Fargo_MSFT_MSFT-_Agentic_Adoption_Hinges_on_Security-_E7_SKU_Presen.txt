Our Call
Leveraging its signif E3 + E5 installed base, MSFT's new E7 SKU + merchandising muscle 
could prove a catalyst for broader M365 Copilot & Agent 365 adoption into C2H26. We est 
325M seats addressable, inc. 150M M365E3/E5 or $100B potential upsell opp.
 
Initial Thoughts
Agent 365, E7 'Frontier Suite' SKU, and Copilot Wave 3: MSFT this morning announced 
a trio of M365 advancements:
1. Agent 365 announced at $15 per user per month, a control plane for managing 
agents. New SKU will be generally available on May 1, 2026.
2. New E7 SKU announced at $99 per user per month, incl M365 E5, Entra Suite, 
Agent 365, and M365 Copilot. New SKU will be generally available on May 1, 2026.
3. M365 Copilot Wave 3 released, built in "close collaboration" with Anthropic's Cowork.
 
 
Expect E7 bundle upsells drive M365 Copilot, Agent 365 adoption; price hikes timely:
New Agent 365 offer brings agent registry/agent ID (in Entra), observability, risk signals, 
etc.—all important prerequisites for agentic adoption. Similar to M365 Copilot, see Agent 
365 adoption as unlikely standalone (via $30/mo, $15/mo SKUs) and instead expect 
both see accelerated adoption via E7 SKU. Note: recent M365 price hikes already force 
enterprise negotiations in 2H26, creating NT adoption catalyst.
 
Quantifying E7 opp: est ~325M enterprise seats, incl ~150M E3/E5 seats; est E7 = 
$100B annual upsell opp: See existing E3/E5 seats as highest propensity to adopt E7. 
Recall MSFT rolled out M365 E3/E5 suites on Aug 1, 2017, disclosed ~45M E5 seats / 
12% of installed base by Jun 30, 2022, and disclosed 160M seats across "premium" SKUs
Sep 21, 2023. Our SKU mapping suggests MSFT now at ~150M seats across E3/E5; 
uplift across E3/E5 = $96B annually. See our prior SKU ests as of Jul 2023 and Jun 2025.

Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks
Microsoft Corporation (MSFT)
Investment Thesis
We still see a bright future ahead for Microsoft, driven by continued growth prospects in huge categories of IT spend, ability to further 
monetize strong positioning in multiple end markets, and a financial profile that continues to exhibit durable margin expansion. We 
acknowledge shares are trading near historical highs, but think this is justified given its early AI lead and strong incumbent position in a 
tight market, esp. favorable in the current environment. We rate shares Overweight.
Target Price Valuation for MSFT
Our $615 PT is derived from 30x P/E on our Fwd NTM estimates, warranted given the unprecedented scale + durability of this biz and 
series of favorable LT tailwinds still ahead. The current AI-focused capex build cycle continues to affect FCF multiples; therefore, we remain 
focused on P/E multiples in the interim.
Risks to Our Price Target and Rating for MSFT
Downside risks to our price target include: (1) Competition—Increasing competition, particularly from large tech vendors such as Google, 
Facebook, Amazon, among others; (2) Increasing regulatory scrutiny—Technology companies have increasingly seen scrutiny from 
regulatory agencies and governments globally over the past several years, specifically, antitrust concerns; (3) Cybersecurity Risks; and (4) 
Margin compression—from Azure/OpenAI investments.
